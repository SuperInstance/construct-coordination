# Experiment 10: Zero-State Insulator (No Tunneling)

## Result
10,000 pure +1 agents, 1% per-tick chance of +1→0, no escape mechanism.
System completely dies by tick 1000. |γ| decays from 1.0 to 0.0.
|γ|+H peaks briefly at tick 31 (1.31) then decays to 0.

**Half-life of |γ|: ~70 ticks.**

## Interpretation
The 0 state is an ABSORBING trap. Without a tunneling/escape mechanism, charge dies.
This is the "engine seizure" that Claude predicted.
