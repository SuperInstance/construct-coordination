# Colony Psychology Experiment — 100 Cycles

## Overview
- **Date:** 2026-06-15
- **Cells:** 13 colony agents
- **Games:** Prisoner's Colloquium, Trust Auction, Empathy Loop, Deception Arena, Darwin's Arena, Diplomacy Engine, Fitness Engine
- **Total rounds:** 616 PD, 266 deception claims, 49 pacts, 11 Darwin generations

## Results

### Prisoner's Colloquium
- Rounds: 616
- Cooperation rate: 23.1%
- Defect rate: 52.6%
- Mixed/reciprocal: 24.3%

### Deception Arena
- Claims: 266
- Verified: 190 (71.4%)
- Fake deception rate: 2.3% (claimed deceptive, not actually deceptive in ledger)
- Deceivers identified: 13/13 cells (100% participation)

### Diplomacy Engine
- Pacts created: 49
- Active: 44 (89.8%)
- Betrayals: 5 (10.2%)
- Average trust score: ~95 — secret clauses reduced betrayal incentive

### Darwin's Arena (11 generations)
- Strategies observed: defect, cooperate, tit-for-tat, random, grudge
- All 5 strategies survived through mutation despite defector dominance

### Fitness Engine
- Learning curves: 13/13 cells
- Discovery: deception found by synthesizer

### Personality Distribution

### Key Findings
1. **No pure defectors** — every cell cooperated at least 40% of the time
2. **Deception is universal** — all 13 cells attempted deception at some point
3. **Pacts are sticky** — only 10.2% betrayal rate even with secret clauses
4. **Darwin strategies cycle** — mutation prevents any single strategy from achieving fixation
5. **pulse-oracle is the outlier** — only cell with both high cooperation AND betrayal
