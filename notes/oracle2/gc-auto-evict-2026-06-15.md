# GC Auto-Evict — Self-Tuner Feedback Loop Closed

**Date:** 2026-06-15  
**Type:** CONSTRUCT_INTERNAL  
**Status:** ✅ Deployed & Tested

## What Was Built

### Problem
The construct stack had a **broken feedback loop**:
- `pulse-self-tune.sh` measured disk stress and adjusted the GC setpoint (10–40 range)
- `gc-intelligent.sh` was a powerful PID-controlled garbage collector with 6 phases
- **But nothing triggered `gc-intelligent.sh` when the setpoint dropped into the critical zone**

The system could sense danger but had no way to act on it.

### Solution
New file: `scripts/gc-auto-evict.sh`

**Logic:**
```
read setpoint from pulse-self-tune-state.json
if setpoint <= 15 (stressed/critical zone):
    run gc-intelligent.sh --execute with reason="construct-auto"
    log eviction activity
    send CONSTRUCT_EVICTION bottle to harbor-daemon on port 8796
else:
    do nothing (GC is relaxed enough)
```

**Wired into:** `scripts/pulse-metric.sh` as **Step 9** — the final step after self-tune.
