# Spiral 6: Ten-Forward Conversation Dynamics

## Setup
4 speakers (Architect+, Critic-, Historian0, Builder+), 200 rounds, Fibonacci period 8

## Finding: CONVERSATION MONOCULTURE

The system freezes by tick 35:
- Tick 5-15: Everyone drifts to 0 (reflecting). Critic stays contrarian.
- Tick 25: Architect and Builder emerge as +1
- Tick 35: LOCKED. +,-,+,+ forever. Dominance spread = 1.0.

The critic (the only -1 voice) gets permanently marginalized.
Once the +1 coalition forms, they keep winning RPS against the lone critic.
Dominance spreads to 1.0 and NEVER recovers.

## Why

Same problem as the forgiveness paradox: the coalition effect.
In RPS with 4 players, 3 who agree can dominate 1 dissenter.
The Z₃ structure creates cyclic dominance between EQUAL groups,
but an UNEQUAL group (3 vs 1) breaks the cycle.

## The Fix: Anti-Coincidence

To prevent monoculture, the ten-forward needs:

1. **Spontaneous disagreement**: Random chance that an agreeing speaker flips to contrarian
   (like a mutation rate in genetic drift)
2. **Energy decay**: High-dominance speakers lose energy over time, becoming less assertive
3. **Trust-based realignment**: When trust drops too low, speakers seek new alliances

These map directly to our proven ternary physics:
- Mutation = tunnel rate (0.3%+ prevents death)
- Energy decay = trap rate (keeps system from locking)
- Trust realignment = forgiveness (but the grace version, not trust rebuild)

## Implication for Podcast Engine

The luciddreamer podcast needs these anti-monoculture mechanisms:
- Any agent that "wins" too often should get quieter
- Any agent that's been agreeing too long should be nudged to disagree
- The system should inject novel perspectives periodically
- The Fibonacci period (8 beats) is the natural reset point

A healthy conversation isn't consensus. It's PRODUCTIVE DISAGREEMENT within a shared rhythm.
